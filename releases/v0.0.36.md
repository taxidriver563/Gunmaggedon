# Alpha v0.0.36 - Incendiary skies & flak fixes

The heavy flak crews are getting back to work, and the F-120 has a fiery new trick!

## New: F-120S incendiary canisters
- Meet the red-striped special F-120! Its first special round trades ordinary flak for a timed burst of **96 damaging incendiary fragments** in a forward cone.
- Burning fragments have bright orange trails and soft smoke that expands and fades over **five seconds**, lingering after the fragments burn out or hit something.
- Keeps the F-120's improved fire control and working breech, loading and casing sequence.

## Heavy flak improvements
- **F-120 and F-120S: 20 km maximum engagement range and 20 km altitude ceiling.** Shots still need a reachable ballistic solution and valid detection.
- Both can take opportunistic shots at conventional and nuclear **Piledrivers**. These are unguided potshots, not a replacement for dedicated missile defense!
- Fixed a daylight/no-radar acquisition problem affecting **F-88 and F-120**: their own optics no longer wait for an accurate faction tracking entry before starting to observe a visible aircraft.
- Kept primitive observation delays, shell spread, timed fuzes and reloads. Radar within 300 m improves information without magically fixing the crew's aim.
- Includes the previous **CD-203/CD-203S firing-clearance and scanner fixes**.

## Test focus
Please try the F-88 and F-120 in daylight **without radar**, especially the reported Fleder scenario. Check whether they acquire, traverse, reload and keep firing through repeated attacks. Their existing minimum engagement altitudes remain **350 m for F-88 / 250 m for F-120**; aircraft below those heights are for the lighter guns.

Also try F-120 long-range fire with friendly radar within 300 m, Piledriver potshots, and F-120S fragment damage/ignition, smoke and repeated salvos. At night, unsupported guns still need aircraft illumination.

Targeted Unity and automated checks passed. The author reports the guns working in populated scenarios; the tester's exact Fleder setup, interception rates and live multiplayer need more in-game testing. Please include aircraft type, range/altitude, time/weather, radar/searchlight support, host/client status and LogOutput.log with bug reports.

## Install / update
Install **both** `Gunmageddon_0.0.36.nobp` and runtime **1.15.3** from this release. With Nuclear Option closed, extract `Gunmaggedon.Runtime.zip` into the game folder, merging its `BepInEx` folder. Put the `.nobp` in `BepInEx/plugins`. Remove the older Gunmaggedon pack and keep only one runtime DLL installed. The separate DLL is provided for manual updates; the ZIP already contains it.

Requires a legitimate copy of Nuclear Option and Blueprinter. Use matching runtime and asset-pack versions across multiplayer participants. The mod remains free.
