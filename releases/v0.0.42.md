# Alpha v0.0.42 — Naval reinforcements & more wreckage!

More guns, more wreckage, and a little more warning before the railguns ruin your day!

## New guns on the coast

- **DP-127:** a modern destroyer gun on a concrete emplacement. Advanced fire control and guided impact shells engage ships and ground targets, while guided proximity shells handle aircraft, out to **15 km**.
- **C-127:** an exposed Old Guard surface gun with working traverse cranks, breech, rammer and casing ejection. Its reload has to finish before it can fire again.
- **C-127T:** the housed twin version adds secondary timed-flak fire against aircraft. Surface targets remain its priority, and each barrel reloads independently.
- **C-127A, C-127B and C-127C:** three fortified bunker variants. The wide C-127C houses two exposed guns; each bunker has firing arcs that fit its gun ports.

The C-127 family uses unguided shells and estimated optical ranging: **12 km against surface targets**, with the twin's secondary AA limited to **6.5 km**. These are Old Guard crews, not perfect tracking computers.

## Railguns: warning, commitment and the old monster

- Player aircraft now receive a dedicated **Railgun [TRACK] / [CHARGE] / [FIRE]** HUD warning with custom tones. Slow tracking pulses build into faster charging pulses and a sustained imminent-fire tone, with a properly fitted dark warning background.
- **R-230 and balanced R-230N** now wait five seconds before reacting to a newly acquired valid target.
- **R-230N** tracks its predicted burst point for five seconds, then freezes that solution for the final three-second charge. The single-shot capacitor and longer recharge replace its rapid three-shot bank. Get away from the marked area once it commits—its muzzle velocity has not been reduced!
- Nuclear projectiles leave much larger, clearer **glassy pressure rings** along their flight path.
- **R-230N-L** brings back the pre-balance menace for anyone who missed it: faster acquisition, continuous tracking through charge, moving-target proximity and the original three-cell bank. It keeps the current warnings and pressure-ring visuals.

## Heavy flak can fight back on the ground

- Regular **F-88 and F-120** automatically switch to direct-fire AP against visible hostile vehicles and buildings when aerial engagements do not take priority.
- Ground engagement range is **4 km for F-88 / 6 km for F-120**. They use their existing ammunition and reload sequence; there is no separate free AP reserve.
- **F-120S** keeps its special incendiary loadout.

## Wrecks and fixes

- Added **15 burnt-out wreck prefabs** for the emplacements and trailers that were missing them. They reuse the original paint and normal maps with shared scorch materials and reduced detail at distance.
- Fixed moving trailer wrecks losing their physics too early, plus wreck cleanup at map edges.
- Fixed C-127 multiplayer shells launching along stale displayed aim, and housed-twin recoil failing to resume after idle.
- Stopped destroyed C-127s from continuing their loading presentation or ejecting an old casing after repair.
- Fixed missing armor/ground impact debris and improved wreck-shader renderer compatibility.
- Made the C-127 animation references self-contained in the production asset folder.

## Install / update

Install **both runtime 1.22.1 and `Gunmageddon_0.0.42.nobp`** from this release. With Nuclear Option closed, extract the runtime ZIP into the game folder, merging its `BepInEx` folder. Put the `.nobp` in `BepInEx/plugins`, remove the older Gunmaggedon pack, and keep only one runtime DLL installed.

**Multiplayer participants must all use this matching runtime and pack. This update uses protocol 10; mixed older versions are incompatible.** Requires a legitimate copy of Nuclear Option, BepInEx and Blueprinter. Gunmaggedon remains free.

This is still an alpha. Sustained missions, live wreck decay/repair and multiplayer reconnects need more playtesting. Please include `LogOutput.log`, the affected unit and host/client status when reporting bugs.
