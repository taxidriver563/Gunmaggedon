Provisional combat defaults
Unguided native BulletSim127mm HE:850m/s, gravity1, drag0.00001,
900pierce/4.5blast,200m minimum,12km surface range. Twin AA maximum6.5km.
Ships, land vehicles and buildings count as surface targets. No missile role.
Exposed variants reject aircraft in selection and all firing gates.
Surface targets always outrank eligible aircraft on the twin.
Surface motion comes from half-second optical samples with estimated velocity,
initial range error and bounded observed-miss correction; no perfect rigidbody
lead. New contacts reset ranging knowledge and require fresh observations.
AA reuses primitive noisy area-barrage estimates and timed fragmentation,
not guided/proximity shells. Native direct kinetic hits remain possible.
Traverse12deg/s, elevation8deg/s. Twin alternates barrels with0.35sec spacing;
each barrel has its own5.17sec reload and100round starting supply.
Day/night, actual native LOS, smoke, faction and exact range checks apply.
In darkness a known ship contact can request existing coastal searchlight
support. Broad grid identity cannot supply an unseen ship's actual position.
No parachute flares or new configuration added to C127.

Mechanics and presentation
Exposed reload uses the supplied GIF's staged5.17sec clip: closed-breech recoil,
wedge opening, spent-case extraction/side clearance, fresh projectile+case,
single ram stroke, secured breech and rammer withdrawal. The full cycle gates
native ammunition. Exactly one pooled physical spent case after clearance.
This is the C127 reference sequence, NOT the F88-specific two-second pause.
Exposed recoil is driven once by its custom clock; housed barrels keep native
independent recoil. Both twin pitch branches follow the controller. Cranks
follow traverse/elevation, with distant cosmetic work skipped. Reload clocks,
readiness and gameplay muzzle/recoil transforms continue at distant LODs.
Muzzle flash/smoke reuse owned F88 emitters; native cannon audio is remapped
at load and uses the existing343m/s travelling-audio system. Existing impact
templates/pools remain shared, without changing their scale globally.
Physical casing uses the user's modeled case; no stock casing mesh copied.
