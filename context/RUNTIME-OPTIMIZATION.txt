Gunmaggedon runtime optimization pass
2026-10-23 | runtime 1.10.0 | unchanged asset pack 0.0.26

Scope
All six implementation areas are integrated in the combined runtime. No new
configuration, network messages, prefab components, damage settings or asset
pack changes. Current LOD groups remain mandatory and unchanged.

Ownership and implementation
- src/Optimization/RuntimePool.cs: active leases with monotonically changing
  IDs, stale/double-release rejection, explicit reset, unlimited active growth.
  Idle retention: audio 128, trails 256, clusterlet heads 512, flights 16,
  opening/water/land particle templates 128 each. Excess is destroyed only on
  release. Mission teardown intentionally destroys active effects too.
- ClusterVisualPools and SpecialPayloadFlight separate head/controller reuse
  from frozen trailing histories. Returned flights are untracked from network
  lists before reuse. Particle state is explicitly stopped/cleared.
- TravellingAudio centralizes delay/expiry processing, with two reusable voices
  per emitter. Clip/settings snapshots and original random selection order are
  preserved; 343 m/s travel, moving listener/camera and near/far mixing remain.
- TrailService owns one camera callback pair. SpottingTrail keeps forwarding
  methods, uses ring samples and reusable bulk-upload buffers. Sampling cadence,
  launch anchor, moving head, clipping, camera-facing alignment, colors and
  four-second ranging lifetime are preserved. Pool registration and release
  are explicit, including editor fixtures where lifecycle callbacks may not run.
- SharedWorldQueries caches broad, read-only snapshots by global 250m cells,
  radius bucket, rendered frame and physics timestamp. Queries are expanded to
  cover the entire cell. Faction contact queries are separately partitioned.
  Consumers still apply their exact evidence, faction, range, operational radar,
  smoke, illumination and visibility gates. Inclusive 300m radar support remains.
- RemovalBuffer scratch lists replace recurring dictionary cleanup arrays.
  One-second maintenance handles ordinary stale entries; shot retirement,
  destroyed-owner checks, reservations, profile ownership and shutdown remain
  immediate. Nested cleanup leases do not share an in-use scratch list.
- F88 reload clock advances once per timestamp before the native gun and via
  LateUpdate fallback. Pose writes happen once per rendered frame, with explicit
  initialization/secure-state exceptions. The two-second clearance pause and
  exactly-once casing handoff remain; native muzzle recoil remains active.
- SharedShipBounds discovers ship colliders every 0.25 seconds and aggregates
  current enabled/active/non-trigger bounds once per physics timestamp. Centers
  are stored globally for floating-origin rebases. Per-submunition previous
  hull centers, swept scans, obstruction/smoke checks and reservations remain
  independent. No scan ticks are staggered or skipped.
- OptimizationRuntime clears pools, caches, listener/audio-template references
  and central trail callbacks on mission-origin replacement or plugin shutdown.

Validation
All 14 standalone SDK suites passed on final source. The new optimization
suite contributes 347 assertions against production pool/query/trail/audio
logic with deterministic Unity transport doubles. It includes randomized cell
coverage, boundary/live radar checks, global rebasing, stale leases, reset
failure, pooled trails and propagation behavior. These doubles do not certify
native audio output or mission presentation.

Unity 2022.3.62f2 editor checks ran sequentially through a temporary editor-only
filesystem bridge after Assets > Refresh. No scene/prefab was saved by the
bridge. Current source was compiled into a uniquely named validation helper.
Results: 10 passing groups, 1,568 checks total:
  static review 34; searchlight/coastal 101; F88 rig 38; effects 30;
  barrage 34; reload 53; live L25 trailer 444; LOD 800; fragments 11;
  optimization 23.
The new native tests exercise actual LineRenderer bulk counts, slow head and
anchor, clipping, camera-time rebasing, frozen expiry, reuse, ring growth,
520 simultaneous heads versus 512 idle retention, stale leases, collider
bounds, and three active-effect teardown/restart cycles.

Existing unrelated failure remains: L25RigRegression expects the preview rig's
shared one-in-three tracer phase, while the staged L25_Turret_Rig feeds are set
to 1. The operational L25 trailer remains at 3 and its 444 checks pass. The
preview setting was not overwritten. Do not report the entire runner as green.

LOD: static audit of seven unit prefabs confirms three descending thresholds
per physical model; the SPECIAL's inactive bomb template has its own group.
The 800 native LOD checks cover renderer membership, cheaper meshes, materials,
near/far cosmetic gating and reload continuation. Mission transitions and
remote multiplayer presentation remain unverified.

Performance evidence and limits
Final native Unity editor microbenchmark: 50 batches of 100 trail lifecycles,
after 20 warmups, comparing pre-optimization 1.9.9 against candidate source.
  Before: median 2.7743 ms, p95 3.4466 ms per 100.
  After:  median 0.9461 ms, p95 1.0942 ms per 100.
Candidate includes reflection retirement overhead. This is editor object/trail
churn, not combat CPU or FPS. Unity Mono's thread allocation counter failed an
allocation probe; native allocation results are NULL/unavailable, not zero.

Final synthetic .NET pool-transport benchmark: 100 batches of 1,000 operations.
  Before: median 0.0604 ms, p95 0.1466 ms; 28,000,448 managed bytes total.
  After:  median 0.0440 ms, p95 0.0516 ms; 0 managed bytes after warmup.
This omits Unity native creation/rendering and cannot establish Unity GC or FPS.

Stage-specific baseline/candidate combat benchmarks were NOT collected. Full
mission profiling remains outstanding for clustered searchlights/F88s,
simultaneous cluster salvos, overlapping top-attack scans, and actual mission
restarts. The implemented stages have combined regression coverage; they do
not have separately measured mission-performance certifications.

Diagnostics and reproduction
build.ps1 -Diagnostics emits development-only counters and profiler samples:
PoolCreates, GridQueries, BoundsBuilds, TrailUploads, ReloadPoseWrites and
Gunmaggedon GridQuery/ShipBounds/Trails/TravellingAudio scopes. Per-scope
AllocatedBytes counters are available only if a working GC thread-allocation
counter passes its startup probe. Otherwise use Unity Profiler GC.Alloc.
Release builds compile out instrumentation calls. Diagnostic instrumentation
itself has overhead; do not compare an instrumented candidate to release baseline.

For remaining benchmarks: use the same mission, camera route, unit counts,
weather/time, duration and machine for both DLLs. Warm up, capture main-thread
CPU median/p95 and GC.Alloc, count pool creation and broad queries, repeat each
scenario and verify effects/damage/ammunition before interpreting timings.
Keep asset pack 0.0.26 fixed. Capture restarts with active delayed audio and
detached trails; verify counts return to zero and leases cannot survive restart.
