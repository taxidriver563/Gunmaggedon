Scope is the mechanical turret, ahead of the heavy-flak behavior discussion.
The original FBX, mesh and material assets are preserved. The imported instance
was unpacked before reparenting, and all ten saved mesh matrices were verified
against the original pose. No new mission unit/weapon definition is registered.
Native Turret and Gun components are present but disabled, with no owner or
ammunition definition and the gun on Safety. This prefab is a staged rig, not
yet an operational mission unit. Firing/shell/fuze behavior remains future work.

Hierarchy:
  Model: base, supports, wheels (fixed carriage)
  F88YawPivot: traverse, actualtraverse, PitchCrankPivot, YawCrankPivot
    PitchPivot: gun (cradle), RecoilPivot
      RecoilPivot: barrel, Muzzle, BreechSlidePivot, BreechSlideRest
        BreechSlidePivot: breech

Yaw axis is local Y at the turntable, elevation local X at the side trunnions.
The trunnion uses the authored gun origin (0,2.885,-1.4473), confirmed by the
visible side-ring vertices. Muzzle is root local (0,2.985,8.25), facing +Z.
Recoil uses a zero-rest wrapper, 0.55m travel, with muzzle and breech following.
The breech moves 0.36m along gun-local +X, so right remains right while aiming.
It stays closed through initial recoil, opens, holds for loading, then closes
before reload completes. Its original height/depth offsets are preserved.

Provisional mechanical settings: 12 degrees/s traverse, 10 degrees/s elevation,
-5 to +85 degrees elevation. Four-second reload is a preview/presentation value,
not a finalized fire-control or ammunition design. Cranks use the L40 gearing:
left = -12 times pitch change, right = 6 times yaw change. They stop with aim.

The 8-second legacy Animation preview shows traverse, elevation, recoil and
breech opening/closing. Select the scene F88, open Window > Animation > Animation
and choose F88_Mechanism_Preview to scrub/play it. Auto-play is disabled, and the
clip finishes at rest. The scene is saved at its original neutral pose.

when they are integrated later. It follows actual native reload state and aiming;
no helper MonoBehaviour is serialized into the prefab. The installed game remains
Runtime 1.8.9 / pack 0.0.19. No game pack was rebuilt or installed for this rig task.
