Engagement and threat policy

does not require an existing faction contact or nearby radar/searchlight.

ballisticMissile1 and ballisticMissile1_tacNuke. Ground/ships/ordinary missiles,
friendlies, inactive/destroyed/disabled units are excluded. Native LOS and smoke
occlusion rechecked at acquisition and firing. No firing through terrain.
Strict priority tiers:
  3: terminal Piledriver
  2: other visible Piledriver
  1: strategic bomber (FastBomber1/Alkyon, Darkreach, or strategic aircraft flag)
  0: other aircraft, ranked by native unit value, anti-surface role, closing speed
     and distance, with a small current-target retention bonus.

sea level. This is an explicit provisional
gameplay criterion, not a native flight-phase flag. Acquisition refreshes every
.2 seconds; ordinary aircraft remain eligible when no higher-tier target is visible.
