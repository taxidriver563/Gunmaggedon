CD-203 concrete resistance — 2026-10-24

Both standard and SPECIAL emplacements previously used one 15x8x15 unarmored
root BoxCollider with 100 HP and armor/tolerances 0/1 for the entire emplacement.
This treated the concrete and exposed weapon identically and filled the open
pit with a coarse collision box.

The concrete now uses its original 172-vertex mesh as a nonconvex static
MeshCollider, with a same-object native UnitPart. The traverse, gun body and
barrel each have an articulated local mesh-bounds BoxCollider and native
UnitPart retaining the original unarmored damage settings. The root coarse box
is removed so it cannot intercept hits meant for the exposed gun/open pit.
Part references point to their own Building; native critical damage still
disables the emplacement. Added parts carry no physical mass and do not detach
independently. No custom damage hooks or runtime balance changes were added.

Concrete armor / tolerance:
  Pierce armor 300; pierce tolerance 6.
  Blast armor 150; blast tolerance 4.
  Fire armor 100; fire tolerance 10.
  Overpressure limit 30. Native starting health remains 100.
These are provisional game balance values, not real-world concrete specs.
For identical incoming post-protection blast inputs, concrete takes 25% of its
former native HP loss. Native blast armor also participates in blast protection.
Light penetrating/fire hits are resisted; sufficiently heavy direct penetration
still destroys the emplacement. Exposed gun parts retain armor 0/tolerances 1.
No firing, targeting, ammunition, muzzle, recoil or material changes.
