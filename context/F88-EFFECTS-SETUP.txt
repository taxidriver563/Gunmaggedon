F88 muzzle flash, custom audio and lingering flak - 2026-10-21

Updated live asset: Assets/Blueprinter/Mods/Gunmageddon/F88_Turret_Rig.prefab.
New effect asset: Assets/Blueprinter/Mods/Gunmageddon/F88_Flak_Airburst.prefab.
Setup tool: GunmaggedonRuntime/tools/SetupF88Effects.cs (repeatable).
Checks: GunmaggedonRuntime/tests/regression/F88EffectsRegression.cs.

The recoil-mounted Muzzle now owns F88MuzzleFlash and F88MuzzleSmoke, copied from
the native-compatible L40 particle setup. Both are registered in Gun.muzzleParticles,
aligned with local +Z, non-looping and not played on load. Flash size is doubled
from the L40 starting effect (1.8m maximum), with its brief 0.05-second lifetime.
The flash and smoke move with barrel recoil and turret elevation/traverse.

Firing: 88mm_Cannon_Fire_Close.mp3, exactly one enabled AudioSource and one
fireSounds entry. Rotary start/sustained/end layers remain empty. This preserves
the game's single-shot routing. The native Gun.ShotSound callback was exercised,
with PlayOneShot intercepted to verify the actual clip selection.

Airburst: a separate copy of the stock airburst_57mm effect, assigned to the gun's
selfDestructEffect. Its root audio source uses Flak_Explosion.mp3; the native
PrefabEffect.Play callback was exercised to verify the selected explosion clip.
Both imported clips decoded non-silent sample data. Sources are spatial,
non-looping, and not played on load; provisional maximum sound distance is 10km.

Only smoke lifetimes are extended by 25 percent:
- Initial puff: stock 5-10s becomes 6.25-12.5s.
- Persistent smoke: stock 10-25s becomes 12.5-31.25s.
Flash, sparks and fragments retain stock timing. Emission rates and burst density
are unchanged. The setup derives lifetimes from stock on each run, so repeating
it does not compound the extension. Other guns' native effects are untouched.

Validation: 30 effects checks plus all 37 F88 mechanical rig checks passed.
Actual particle simulation confirmed lingering smoke at 11s, no lingering flash,
and complete particle expiration by 34s. A three-second puff was rendered and
visually inspected. Saved rig remains free of missing scripts and keeps its
native combat callbacks disabled, pending the heavy-flak/unit integration work.
This is prepared in Unity, not installed as a new mission-ready game pack.
