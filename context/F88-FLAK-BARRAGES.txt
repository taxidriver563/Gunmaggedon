Behavior
- Candidate identities come from faction-known enemy aircraft contacts.
- Optical observations require clear sight within 8km, including mod smoke.
  In low light, a friendly searchlight must actually illuminate the aircraft.
- Friendly operational, activated, unjammed radar within 300m can supply its
  detected aircraft contacts. The distance check is inclusive and revalidated.
- Optical samples arrive every 1.5s; radar samples every 0.35s. Position error
  and velocity estimation improve with radar knowledge. Neither aircraft rigid
  body velocity nor acceleration is read as perfect fire-control information.
- Velocity estimates require an observation baseline. Contact loss permits at
  most six seconds of extrapolation before firing stops.
- A barrage commits an observed position/movement estimate for three shots or
  twelve seconds. New observations do not rewrite that committed estimate.
  The predicted region advances along its committed velocity; current target
  maneuvers are not followed until the next barrage.
- Guns supporting the same contact within 1.5km share a committed estimate and
  cover overlapping sectors. Each shell samples a substantial horizontal and
  vertical volume rather than a point on the aircraft.
- Shell dispersion is fixed at 0.65 degrees and fuze error at +/-0.18s. Separate
  random streams keep mechanical fuze errors independent of radar observations.
- Gravity/drag ballistics solve to the selected region. Aim/fuze preparation
  converges before firing, runs twice per second, and also throttles failed
  solves. The mechanical turret still moves at 12 degrees/s yaw, 10 degrees/s
  elevation. Native firing callbacks run at their normal fixed-step cadence.
- Timed shells have no target/proximity fuse, homing, acceleration prediction,
  in-flight corrections or proximity triggers. Outgoing bullet tracers are
  disabled; muzzle flashes and visible airbursts provide shot presentation.

Damage and presentation
- Native BulletSim is retained for gravity, drag, collisions and shell expiry.
  Native timed expiry is visual-only, so a tracked authoritative expiry emits
  exactly one native DamageEffects.BlastFrag event at the actual shell position.
- Remote visual bullets and received effect messages cannot cause damage.
- Lingering smoke has no damage behavior. Independent pooled puffs preserve
  earlier bursts; at most 128 instances are retained. A 512-flight guard bounds
  tracked shells. Datum-relative particle simulation preserves origin rebasing.
- The user's muzzle flash, 88mm firing sound, flak explosion sound, moving
  handles, recoil and right-sliding breech remain assigned. Airburst sound uses
  the existing travelling-audio helper. Native one-shot firing audio remains.
- New Mirage messages replicate gun pose and authoritative burst presentation
  to compatible peers; serializers are explicit, with a versioned handshake.
  Late joiners receive subsequent poses/bursts, not a history of old smoke puffs.

Validation

- Across 200 deterministic straight-flight trials, mean position knowledge
  error was 35.4m with radar versus 106.3m optical; 5s extrapolation error was
  103.7m versus 200.0m. Identical fuze-error streams were verified independently.
- 219 fresh-current-build Unity checks passed: 34 review, 84 coastal/searchlight,
  37 F88 rig, 30 F88 effects and 34 F88 flak integration checks.
- Integration exercised the actual native Gun.Fire entry without a direct
  target and actual Bullet.TrajectoryTrace timed expiry, capturing exactly one
  fragmentation callback. It checked no proximity detonation, no remote damage,
  radar distance/faction/state gates, dark optical rejection, committed areas,
  Mirage serializer round trips, simultaneous puffs and pool reuse.
- Damage-event tests intercept the native damage callback; this is not a live
  aircraft HP/balance measurement. No full mission or two-peer test was run.
  Representative mission FPS and combat effectiveness remain unmeasured.
- Repeated editor tests need a clean script domain after replacing dynamically
  loaded MonoBehaviour test assemblies; the final run used a clean domain.
