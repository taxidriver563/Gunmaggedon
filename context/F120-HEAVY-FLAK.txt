Fire-control profile (provisional gameplay balance, not historical specs)
The modeled sensors represent an optical rangefinder, not an onboard radar.
Maximum engagement 15 km; own optical range 10 km; altitude 250–12,000 m;
minimum engagement 600 m. Nearby friendly active, unjammed radar support uses
the existing inclusive 300 m boundary and contact/visibility/authority gates.
F120 estimates motion from observations; it does not read perfect target velocity.
Optical/radar observation intervals 0.5/0.2 s, shorter four-second area plans,
less observation noise, faster correction, 0.45 degree launch spread and 0.10 s
fuze error distinguish it from F88's older committed barrage behavior. It still
fires unguided timed shells with finite spread and uncertain lead.
Yaw/pitch 20/16 degrees per second. 950 m/s, drag .035, gravity 1, mass 22 kg.
80 rounds, single-round loading; 6.2-second ordered cycle. Trailer mass 12 t.

Fragmentation
240 m radius versus F88's 140 m. Range falloff remains (1-d/r)^.75.
