Mandatory unit LOD policy
Established: 2026-10-22

LOD setup is required for every new Gunmaggedon unit. Existing units must be
audited and upgraded when they are modified. The workspace AGENTS.md contains
the implementation and validation requirements for future work.

A compliant unit has a full-detail near level and a meaningfully cheaper
distant level, plus appropriate final culling. An empty LODGroup, duplicate
full-detail meshes at every level, or a single level followed by culling is
not sufficient. Effects with different visibility needs require separate
management and a documented reason.

Baseline prefab audit, 2026-10-22:
- Standard and special CD-203: one LOD level, culling at 0.01 screen height;
  no progressively simpler model. The special's inactive submunition body
  is outside that group.
- L40, L25, F88 and searchlight trailers: no prefab LODGroup.
- Top-attack bomb: no prefab LODGroup; its mesh is already small.

Retrofit completed in Unity on 2026-10-22: all seven unit prefabs and the F88
and L25 staged rigs now have three model levels. The SPECIAL's inactive bomb
template has a separate group. See UNIT-LOD-RETROFIT.txt for geometry budgets,
cosmetic animation handling, validation and deployment status.
Ordinary camera culling is separate from mesh LOD.

Completion checks:
- Valid enabled LODGroups and correct renderer membership on exported units.
- Actual reduction of distant geometry or small rendered detail.
- Appropriate bounds, thresholds and culling for the unit's role.
- Working recoil, traverse, loading visibility and materials at transitions.
- Separate handling for long-range beams and other effects as appropriate.
- Gameplay remains active regardless of visual LOD.
- Document editor/export checks separately from actual mission testing.
