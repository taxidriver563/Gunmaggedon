Coastal request and visible tracking fix - Runtime 1.8.7

Host/single-player coastal batteries now request illumination every 0.5 seconds
independently of native weapon aiming and firing. A battery needs an operational
friendly searchlight within 2 km and a faction-known enemy ship contact. This
avoids a dark-target rejection preventing the request itself. Unknown ships are
not revealed and gun targets are not assigned by the coordinator.

A lamp can accept a sector cue before confirming a clear sightline, so it visibly
turns toward the request. Actual illumination still requires sustained acquisition
and a clear path, including smoke. Coastal elevation can depress to -30 degrees
for cliff positions. Initial ship-cue wobble now stays inside the acquisition cone.
Aircraft tracking and radar/optical aircraft cues retain priority.

State-change messages in BepInEx/LogOutput.log identify missing nearby lamps,
missing faction-known contacts, battery requests and lamp acceptance.

Validation: 34 current-build review checks, 76 coastal/searchlight Unity checks,
and all nine standalone suites passed. No live mission or multiplayer test was
performed for this change. Uses existing asset pack 0.0.19.
