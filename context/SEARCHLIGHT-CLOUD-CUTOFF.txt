Searchlight hard cloud cutoff - asset pack 0.0.18, helper unchanged at 1.8.3

Replaces the soft extinction tail introduced in pack 0.0.17. Once the beam
enters a cloud with native sampler density greater than 0.15, its scattering
pixels are discarded completely. The first layer intersection is sampled so
points farther along do not reappear because of a gap at a later sample point.
Clear cloud-map gaps and the beam below the layer are preserved.

Runtime weather binding, wind movement, floating-origin anchoring, native
spotlight and lens glare remain unchanged. Targeting and coastal illumination
rules are unchanged. This is a shader/pack update; helper 1.8.3 remains valid.

Validation: shader compiled without messages. GPU regression passed with
below-cloud intensity ratio 1.0, dense-cloud continuation ratio 0.0, thin-cloud
continuation ratio 0.0 and maximum clear-gap/control pixel difference 0.0.
No mission or FPS test was performed for this visual adjustment.
