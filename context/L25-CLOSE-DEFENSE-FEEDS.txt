L25 close-defense profile and independent clip feeds - Runtime 1.9.4

User confirmed: 15 rounds per barrel, reloading independently.
Scope remains the staged turret rig; no spawnable trailer definition or pack
build yet. Installed operational pack remains 0.0.23.

The former shared Gun on PitchPivot is replaced by FeedLeft, FeedMiddle and
FeedRight, each containing its own native Gun, AudioSource, one physical
muzzle and its matching flash/smoke pair. Each native WeaponStation owns one
gun with a distinct station number, preserving separate native ammunition,
reload state and network shot routing for future unit integration.

Settings:
- Maximum range 3500m, also enforced by custom aim/fire authorization.
- Native 25mm ballistics retained; lifetime extended to 14s to reach 3.5km.
- Fifteen rounds per clip, 45 loaded total when the operational unit is stocked.
- Provisional reload duration: two seconds for each gun independently.
- 200rpm per gun; 600rpm aggregate with at least 0.1s between actual shots.
- Round-robin sequence skips empty, reloading or cooling-down guns. With fewer
  ready guns the aggregate rate drops; surviving guns retain their own 200rpm.
- Native Gun owns ammo consumption and reload completion. BeforeGun clears
  stale queued triggers on guns that are not eligible, preventing a skipped
  SpawnBullet call from consuming ammunition or same-tick multi-barrel shots.
- Public RoundsInClip(index) and ReloadSeconds(index) expose the actual native
  state for later presentation/UI work; no new HUD or physical clip models.

Close-defense optical tracking:
- Best profile at or below 750m, progressively worse toward 3500m.
- Optical sample interval grows from 0.12 to 0.5 seconds.
- Velocity-estimate response time grows from 0.18 to 1.8 seconds.
- Acquisition requires multiple observations; minimum delay grows from 0.15
  to 1 second, rather than reading an exact aircraft Rigidbody velocity.
- Aim wobble grows from 0.12 to 1.4 degrees; native launch spread from 0.18
  to 1 degree. Gravity and an approximate drag flight-time card are applied.
- Lost/stale sightings block fire. Bullets remain ordinary ballistic rounds.
This is a tunable first profile; actual combat kill rates have not been measured.
