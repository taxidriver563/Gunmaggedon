(9,567 checks), exported asset/LOD checks. These do not certify sustained live
missions, collision-driven decay/repair, actual multiplayer transport or FPS.

metadata identifies the source and compiler. Private references are excluded.

nine original MP3 bodies are restored only by maintainers rebuilding the pack.
Native assets resolve through Blueprinter's game references, requiring the game.
