F88 shell extraction and loading - Runtime 1.9.2 / pack 0.0.22

Both F88_Turret_Rig and F88_AA_Trailer now use the supplied shell_casing
and shell_loaded meshes from Gunmaggedon F88 AAA Shells.fbx. Their geometry
is centered into unit-scale wrappers, retaining the imported mesh/materials.
They follow elevation and traverse, independently of barrel recoil.

Nominal sequence after firing (seconds; slower frames may extend it):
0.0-0.6: recoil recovery, breech stays shut.
0.6-1.0: breech door slides right.
1.0-1.8: spent casing slides aft out of the breech/loading tray.
1.8: animated case disappears; matching physics prop appears at the same pose.
1.8-3.8: at least two full seconds of empty-breech clearance.
3.8-5.4: complete round moves forward from the tray into the chamber.
5.4-6.0: breech door closes after the round is fully seated.
6.0-6.2: secured dwell, then native ammunition can become loaded again.

The phase clock resets at actual casing handoff. A delayed frame cannot skip
the two-second pause, skip the visible insertion stage, or mark the gun ready
before closure. Native reload time is 6.2 seconds; the runtime holds both
bulletsLoaded and queued shots empty until the physical sequence finishes.
The ordinary initial spawn remains preloaded with its breech closed.
The manual fire gate also requires a mechanically ready gun.

Ejected casings are harmless Rigidbody props with a small BoxCollider. They
use gentle 0.6m/s rearward velocity, inherit carrier motion, ignore their own
gun's colliders, and retire after eight seconds. A shared 64-prop pool bounds
the physics cost. Native ejectionTransform remains null to avoid a second
casing spawn; ejectionPrefab carries the custom casing reference.
Remote native shot events start the same visual sequence without imposing a
client-side reload timer that could block later remote fire events. Late-join
reconstruction of an already active reload is not added in this change.

F88_Mechanism_Preview.anim is now ten seconds long. Its demonstration shot is
at 2.8s, casing handoff at 4.6s, insertion begins at 6.6s, door secured at 9s,
and all aiming transforms return to rest at 10s. Preview does not simulate the
detached physical casing; the runtime performs that handoff.
