Searchlight smooth tracking and acoustic cues - Runtime 1.9.7
Compatible installed asset pack: 0.0.25; no new pack needed.

Targeted aircraft now use damped angular motion (0.25s smoothing, existing
20deg/s yaw and 15deg/s elevation caps). Once an aircraft has actually been
seen inside the beam, the target point refreshes every frame and the search
wobble is removed. This replaces staircase-like cue updates and continued
oscillation during an optical lock. Another radar contact cannot steal that
recent visible lock. After 0.3s without visual contact, the lamp falls back
to its existing coarse cue/search behavior; reporting still requires current
beam contact and a clear sightline. Disabled lamps clear motion velocities.

Engine-noise cues:
- Read native aircraft IEngine RPM ratios once per second using the existing
  candidate list; no additional battlefield-grid scans or per-frame engine scans.
- Engine-off or <=0.15 RPM ratio produces no cue. Audible radius is 3km at low
  running RPM, scaling to 5km at full RPM. Invalid or destroyed engine entries
  are ignored. This is a gameplay estimate of audibility, not an audio-engine
  sound-propagation simulation.
- A cue estimates bearing with several degrees of slowly drifting error,
  elevation error and up to 25% range error. The lamp sweeps that wider sector.
- Radar remains the more precise cue and takes priority over hearing.
- Hearing creates no evidence, detector contact, faction report or illumination
  coverage. Normal sustained optical observation must still acquire the aircraft.
- Existing exclusive aircraft claims apply, leaving other lamps free to search.
- Aircraft priority over coastal support and night/low-light activation remain.

Also corrected pre-acquisition loss: evidence resetting after 2.5 seconds of
blind traversal no longer drops an unconfirmed assignment before its existing
12-second acquisition timeout. Previously a distant search sector could be
abandoned before the lamp had time to turn toward it.

Validation: all 762 current-build Unity checks and all 12 SDK suites passed.
New integration cases cover 3/5km audibility, stopped/friendly engines, rough
cue error, no automatic reports, exclusive tokens, radar priority, continuous
visible-point updates, no phase wobble in a visual lock, bounded damped aiming,
radar retarget suppression and pre-acquisition traversal persistence. Existing
coastal depression/horizon tests now advance realistic 0.02/0.05s steps rather
than artificial 1-20s aim updates. Coastal range/support, L25 and F88 checks pass.

Installed with Nuclear Option closed; previous runtime backed up to
UnitySetupBackup/Searchlight/BeforeSmoothAcoustic. Source/installed hashes match.
An actual mission visual check is still needed. Existing remote-client local
sweep presentation is unchanged; multiplayer target-pose replication is not
introduced by this update.
