Texture reuse / shader
One shared128x128 mipmapped repeating soot texture and one custom URP shader,
Gunmaggedon/WreckScorch. Twenty-five lightweight material variants retain each
original material's paint texture, normal texture and base UV mapping. No new
per-unit wreck texture sets. Scorch is multiplied into the original paint in
the existing forward material pass, with ordinary shadow/depth passes; no
extra overlaid mesh/double-draw or z-fighting layer.
Dark charcoal tint, mottled soot, metallic.05, smoothness.07, no emission.
Concrete uses a lighter tint than metal. Transparent/glowing parts become
opaque charred surfaces. Reuses only the user's existing geometry/textures.
The initial stock URP detail-map approach hit the16sampler limit in some
variants. It was replaced, not shipped. The custom shader samples paint,
normal and shared soot, supports main/additional lighting, fog, screen-space
shadows and Forward+; compiled/exported successfully for the current editor.
Live game quality-setting/platform coverage is not certified.

Models / lifecycle
Static snapshots reuse original near-detail meshes, with drooped barrels,
slight traverse offsets and removed cranks/loading props. Casemate guns stay
within a shallow droop to retain port clearance. Concrete bodies remain
standing; these are burnt-out wrecks, not newly modeled rubble/destruction.
No Gun, Turret, UnitPart, Unit, NetworkIdentity, Animation, Animator, light,
particle or sound components on the new wrecks. Wrecks cannot target/fire.
All fifteen have enabled three-level LODGroups with cheaper distant meshes.
All model renderers are included. Static wreck presentation needs no cosmetic
animation updates. Live unit LODs, transforms, materials and weapons preserved.
