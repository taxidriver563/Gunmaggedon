F120 native damage routing — 2026-10-24

Cause
The F120 setup removed the F88's colliders on its root/turret UnitPart objects,
then created replacement colliders on wheel contacts, pitch/recoil and carriage
collision helpers. Those 13 objects had no IDamageable component. Native
Bullet.TrajectoryTrace, DamageEffects.ArmorPenetrate/BlastFrag/FragTrace resolve
IDamageable on the collider's own GameObject, not an ancestor. Impacts therefore
failed to damage the unit despite its existing critical chassis/turret parts.

Correction
FixF120Damage.cs narrowly migrates the prefab, with a before-change backup.
Each non-trigger collision object now carries a native UnitPart with this unit
and its single Rigidbody assigned. Chassis/wheel/support shapes inherit the
existing chassis armor; pitch/barrel shapes inherit the existing turret armor.
All are critical, as on the existing reference parts. Added parts have zero
mass so total mass/suspension stay unchanged. These helper objects do not detach
or fragment independently (negative structural/integrity thresholds and empty
disintegration arrays); native lethal critical damage still disables/reports
the whole unit. No runtime damage multiplier or invulnerability bypass added.
SetupF120.cs includes the same invariant for future initial setups.
No geometry, collider shape, targeting, firing, recoil, loading, LOD or materials
were changed. Shared carriage and F88 prefab are not migration targets.
